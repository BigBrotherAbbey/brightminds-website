Why we started

[Founder's name] founded Bright Minds after seeing how hard it was for families in Nigeria to find specialist, compassionate support for children with autism and other developmental needs, and how difficult it was to buy the right therapy tools locally.

Today our therapists and educators work alongside parents to help children communicate, learn, play and grow in confidence. Alongside therapy, we source quality tools so progress can continue at home.
