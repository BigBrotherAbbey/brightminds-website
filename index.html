Support for how your child communicates, moves, learns and plays

Every programme starts with getting to know your child: what they enjoy, what they find hard and what matters most to your family.

Speech and language therapy

Helping children understand and use language, from first sounds and words to conversation, including picture-based and device-based communication.

Occupational therapy

Building the everyday skills that make home and school easier: dressing, eating, handwriting, play, and coping when the world feels too loud.

Behaviour therapy

Warm, child-led behaviour support that teaches new skills and reduces the behaviours that get in the way of learning and family life.

Developmental assessment

A careful look at how your child is developing, with clear findings explained in plain language and a written plan you can act on.

Special education support

One-to-one and small-group teaching shaped around how your child learns best, with school-readiness and inclusion support.

Parent coaching

You know your child best. We give you practical strategies for home so progress continues between sessions.

Questions parents ask

Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp.

How much do sessions cost?

Fees depend on the therapies your child needs, which we recommend after the first assessment. We'll give you a clear written breakdown before anything starts, and offer session packages that cost less per session.

Does my child need a diagnosis first?

No. You can come to us with concerns alone. We can carry out screening and assessment, and refer you on if a medical review is needed.

What ages do you work with?

Children from around 18 months to 16 years. Early intervention for toddlers is one of our main areas.

Can we do sessions at home or online?

Yes. Many therapies are available as home visits within Lagos, and parent coaching works well online for families elsewhere.

Do you deliver therapy products outside Lagos?

Yes, across Nigeria. Add items to your order and send it to us on WhatsApp, and we'll confirm the delivery cost to your area.

The first conversation is the hardest. We'll make it easy.

Tell us what you've noticed. We'll listen, meet your child in a calm setting and explain the options clearly, with no pressure.
