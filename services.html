Therapy that meets your child where they are

Evidence-based, play-centred support from qualified therapists, at our centre, at home or online. Fees are set after assessment so you only pay for what your child needs.

Speech and language therapy

Helping children understand and use language, from first sounds and words to conversation, including picture-based and device-based communication.

Ask about speech and language therapy

Often helps with

  • Late talking and unclear speech
  • Understanding and following instructions
  • Picture exchange (PECS) and AAC
  • Feeding and oral-motor skills

Occupational therapy

Building the everyday skills that make home and school easier: dressing, eating, handwriting, play, and coping when the world feels too loud.

Ask about occupational therapy

Often helps with

  • Fine and gross motor skills
  • Sensory processing and regulation
  • Self-care and independence
  • Handwriting readiness

Behaviour therapy

Warm, child-led behaviour support that teaches new skills and reduces the behaviours that get in the way of learning and family life.

Ask about behaviour therapy

Often helps with

  • Individual behaviour plans
  • Positive reinforcement
  • Routines and transitions
  • Measured progress you can see

Developmental assessment

A careful look at how your child is developing, with clear findings explained in plain language and a written plan you can act on.

Ask about developmental assessment

Often helps with

  • Autism screening
  • ADHD screening
  • Developmental milestone review
  • Written report and therapy plan

Special education support

One-to-one and small-group teaching shaped around how your child learns best, with school-readiness and inclusion support.

Ask about special education support

Often helps with

  • Individual education plans
  • Early literacy and numeracy
  • School-readiness programme
  • Working with your child's school

Parent coaching

You know your child best. We give you practical strategies for home so progress continues between sessions.

Ask about parent coaching

Often helps with

  • Parent workshops
  • Home programme coaching
  • Parent support circle
  • Guidance for siblings and family

Where sessions happen

At the centre

Calm, sensory-friendly therapy rooms in Lekki with specialist equipment.

In your home

Therapy in familiar surroundings, so new skills carry into everyday life.

Online

Parent coaching and selected therapies by video call, wherever you live.
